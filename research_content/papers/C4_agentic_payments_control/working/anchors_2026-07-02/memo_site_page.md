## Opening prose (verbatim extract)

> x402 Research memo, version 1, June 11, 2026. All on-chain figures measured June 11, 2026 (UTC); the x402 ecosystem moves weekly. Full title: "The Control Layer of Agentic Payments: Facilitator Concentration, Subsidy Pipelines, and Settlement Accountability in x402." Agentic payments have a measurement literature and a leaderboard culture, but no one measures the control layer: the small set of facilitator operators through whom x402 settlement actually routes, and the accountability structure of the composition chain those operators sit in. This memo reports the first such measurement, across the three chains that carry materially all x402 activity (Base, Polygon, Solana), using the same gateway-concentration method the research program applied to stablecoin routing in Routing the Dollar.
